# Support HitTheKit

HitTheKit is developed as an open-source project. Financial support is intended
to fund verifiable project costs and additional development time; it does not
buy gameplay advantages, governance control, commercial-song rights, a
commercial license, or guaranteed support.

## Funding channel status

GitHub Sponsors is the active primary channel for recurring and one-time
support:

<https://github.com/sponsors/Codewriter90x>

The maintainer's Sponsors profile is public and its payment connection is
active. The repository integration is defined in `.github/FUNDING.yml`. Before
this change is published, the profile, funding link and Sponsor button must be
reviewed from a logged-out session.

## What funding would support

Priority order:

1. Apple Developer signing and notarization costs;
2. additional MIDI modules, electronic kits, and audio interfaces for
   reproducible compatibility and latency tests;
3. accessibility and clean-machine release validation;
4. website and distribution infrastructure; and
5. focused development time for cross-platform MIDI and learning content.

Funding will not be used to acquire or redistribute unlicensed commercial
music, transcriptions, artwork, or recordings.

## Sponsor tiers

The public profile currently offers three monthly tiers in US dollars:

- **$5 — Supporter:** helps sustain independent open-source development;
- **$15 — Backstage Supporter:** adds optional supporter recognition and
  occasional development or release updates; and
- **$35 — Studio Patron:** adds prominent optional recognition and early access
  to selected public playtest or release-candidate builds.

Custom monthly and one-time support remains available through GitHub Sponsors.
Rewards are digital, low-maintenance, privacy-conscious and independent of GPL
rights. Sponsorship does not purchase guaranteed features, roadmap control,
private GoMyRO access, commercial-song rights or a commercial HitTheKit license.

## Transparency

After project funding is received, the maintainer will publish periodic,
appropriately aggregated information about:

- total project funding received, at a level allowed by the platform;
- major project expenses and their purpose;
- completed work tied to the published roadmap; and
- any change to tiers, fees, or funding channels.

Personal bank, tax, identity, and individual sponsor data will remain private.
Sponsors should consult their own tax adviser; the project makes no claim that
support is tax deductible.

## Other valuable support

Testing a release candidate, reporting a reproducible timing issue, documenting
a drum module, reviewing accessibility, and sharing the project are all useful
ways to help without spending money. Start with [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) and
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
